# Artificing / Arcane Forge

Artificing is the manual, immediate, one-item Equipment crafting system. It consumes the authored ingredient list for exactly one recipe and creates one Equipment item per deliberate craft.

Transmutation is separate: it is repeatable elemental-material production funded by Arcane Echoes. Equipment is not a Transmutation output or job.

The authoritative Equipment recipe definitions remain in `src/game/content/recipes/artificingRecipes.ts`; costs and unlock conditions are unchanged from the prior authored recipes.

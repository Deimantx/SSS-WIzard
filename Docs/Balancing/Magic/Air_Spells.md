# Air spells

**ID:** `air`

**Tagline:** Speed and disruption
**Fragment:** Air Fragment (air-fragment)

## Air Lance

**ID:** `air-lance`

A focused lance of compressed air that strikes the enemy.

**Type:** Damage
**Unlock level:** 2
**Mana cost:** 14
**Cooldown:** 6 s
**Auto-Cast:** Always

**What it does:** 60% of Spell Power Air damage to the opponent
## Quickening

**ID:** `quickening`

A gust that accelerates every Basic Attack.

**Type:** Buff
**Unlock level:** 8
**Mana cost:** 16
**Cooldown:** 12 s
**Auto-Cast:** Always

**What it does:** Apply Quickening to the caster
## Shock Spark

**ID:** `shock-spark`

A spark that leaves the enemy vulnerable to subsequent Air damage.

**Type:** Damage
**Unlock level:** 16
**Mana cost:** 18
**Cooldown:** 8 s
**Auto-Cast:** Always

**What it does:** 45% of Spell Power Air damage to the opponent; Apply Shock to the opponent

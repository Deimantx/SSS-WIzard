# Water spells

**ID:** `water`

**Tagline:** Recovery and protection
**Fragment:** Water Fragment (water-fragment)

## Water Ward

**ID:** `water-ward`

Wraps the wizard in a frequent, flexible barrier.

**Type:** Barrier
**Unlock level:** 2
**Mana cost:** 15
**Cooldown:** 8 s
**Auto-Cast:** when the caster's Barrier is below 10

**What it does:** Grant 70% of Spell Power Barrier to the caster (replacing the current Barrier) for 9 s
## Flow Mend

**ID:** `flow-mend`

A restorative current for a wounded wizard.

**Type:** Heal
**Unlock level:** 8
**Mana cost:** 18
**Cooldown:** 10 s
**Auto-Cast:** when the caster's Health is below 70%

**What it does:** Restore 80% of Spell Power Health to the caster
## Frostbite

**ID:** `frostbite`

Bites into the enemy with cold and slows its combat tempo.

**Type:** Damage
**Unlock level:** 16
**Mana cost:** 22
**Cooldown:** 10 s
**Auto-Cast:** Always

**What it does:** 65% of Spell Power Water damage to the opponent; Apply Chilled to the opponent

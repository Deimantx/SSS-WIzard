## New Mechnics Descriptions:


## Summoning Elemental Guardians:
 - Helps in Combat by Dealing Damage, provides various bonus traits for wizard.
 - 4 Starting elements Fire, Earth, Water, Air.
 - Not ALIVE ENTITY in Combat, this menans they cannot get attacked or die, they have no hp, mana, defence...
 - Requires large mana/s to sustain them in combat. If wizard mana reaches 0, they despawn untill next battle if wizard manages to start new battle with positive mana.
 - Able to upgrade them by leveling their level + ranks which improves their combat capabilities.

## Crystals:
 - Various crystals which provide combat benefits, fit into its coresponding slot on wizard "?body?",  inspired by black desert crystal system.
 - Lots of RNG factor to get them, grindy content: Lootboxes, small chance 0.1% from monsters. Universal Drop almost all zones contain chances to drop them, later acts contain stronger tiers already without combining first.
 - Early simple bonuses +3Spell power, +5Health etc.. later +15spellpower, +50Hp, +3hp per attack and spellcast.
 - Able to combine few crystals + crystal dusts into stronger crystal variations, after that you combine stronger crystals for more stronger ones, this is the loop.

 
## Alchemy:
 - Various potions, elixirs, flasks to help you in combat.
 - Automatation rules where you decide when to you what.
 - Healing potions restores hp, elixirs provide short but strong buffs, flasks long but weak buffs.
 - Limited slots which can be upgraded. 3slots when starting out.
 - Potions, elixirs, flasks never expire or is fully consumed they are endless, but has limited times use inside dungeon, limit resets after you do one full dungeon loop(boss dies)
